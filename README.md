# Food Grade Checker

Build me a website which is used to scan foods. Whenever a person scan a food on that website, the website tells that the food is A grade, B grade, or C grade, or D grade, or even worse. And then a detailed paragraph or something about why it is bad for health, what it contains, what's bad, ऐसा क्या है उसके अंदर जिससे वो बुरा है health के लिए, and what are the alternatives.
